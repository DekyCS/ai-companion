# What Khina takes from the reference photos

Photos: see `CREDITS.md`. They are for shape and colour only.

## From real hedgehogs
- Spines are banded: dark brown at the base, cream at the tip. Together they read as a speckled brown-cream dome.
- The spines lie back, pointing to the rear, and start at the forehead just above the eyes. Face, cheeks and belly are soft fur.
- Face: tapered snout, darker brown around the eyes, cream-beige cheeks, small round dark eyes, a glossy black nose at the very tip, little rounded ears just at the edge of the spines.
- Body: an oval dome, low to the ground; the legs barely show, just small pinkish feet.
- Juveniles (ref-2) are rounder, with a shorter snout and paler spines. That is the direction for "cute".

## Stylised for Khina
- Head almost as big as the body; short rounded snout; big eyes with a white highlight; soft pink cheeks.
- Spines short, soft and thick at the base, so the coat reads as fluffy rather than sharp.
- Natural colours only (warm browns and creams). No clothes, no accessories, and no features borrowed from an existing hedgehog character.
- Built from separate rigid parts (body, head, feet) so the head can tuck in for curling and the feet can waddle.
